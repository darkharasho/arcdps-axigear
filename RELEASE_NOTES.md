# Release Notes

Version v0.2.0 — October 8, 2026

## See your build and gear, not just a list of checks

The window now has two tabs under the problems list.

- **Build** shows the comp's build for your slot: the skill bar, then
  one card per specialization with its background art, the chosen
  traits, and a mark on anything you have set differently.
- **Equipment** shows armor and weapons on the left, and trinkets,
  relic, food and utility on the right. Each piece shows its icon and
  name, its rune or sigil by name, and its infusions on the same row.

Clicking a problem in the list jumps to the tab it belongs to and
makes the slot in question pulse, so you can see what to change.

## Keep several comps and switch between them

Every comp you load is saved. The arcdps options tab lists them, each
with **Use**, **Refresh** and **Unsubscribe**. In the main window,
"Comp:" is now a dropdown, so you can switch comps without opening the
options. Switching uses the saved copy, so it works offline.

The comp you had loaded in v0.1 carries over on its own.

## The API key saves itself

The key saves as soon as you leave the field, and **Test** saves it
first if you changed it. The Save button is gone.

## Smaller fixes

- Food and utility from AxiForge links that list them by item number
  now show their real names and are checked correctly.
